This is the step that brings BF2142 back online. The OpenSpy patches point your game at a working login server, so you can create an account, see servers and join games.

<GuideSummary step={4} time="3 minutes">

Run the Hub as administrator, pick <mark class="ui">OpenSpy</mark> under <mark class="ui">Redirects\*</mark> and click <mark class="ui">Install</mark>. You're done when all four ticks are green.

</GuideSummary>

<details id="openspy">

<summary>What is OpenSpy, and why do I need it?</summary>

Online games like BF2142 rely on a _master server_: it handles your login and soldier data, lists the game servers in your browser, and records your progress. BF2142's master server ran on GameSpy, and it went dark when [GameSpy shut down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GameSpy#Shutdown) in 2014.

[OpenSpy](https://openspy.net/) is an open-source replacement for GameSpy. The OpenSpy patches redirect your game to it, so you can log in and play online again.

</details>

<details id="project-reclamation">

<summary>Who runs the servers?</summary>

Mostly **Project Reclamation**, a community effort that brought BF2142's online features back using OpenSpy. The Reclamation team also makes BF2142 Hub.

<figure><img src="/assets/reclamation_orig.webp" alt="Reclamation servers in the BF2142 server browser" width="563" loading="lazy" /></figure>

There are EU and US servers. EU usually gets busy from around 6 PM GMT, US from around midnight GMT, and weekends are busiest. You'll find Conquest, Conquest Co-op and Titan[^1].

To join, you'll need the OpenSpy patches (this guide) and the [Reclamation map pack](/getting-started/install-map-pack/). The English-speaking community hangs out on their [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/MEwBW9U).

</details>

<details id="gadgets-x26-unlocks">

<summary>Do I get all the unlocks?</summary>

Yes! On OpenSpy, every new soldier starts with all unlocks in singleplayer and multiplayer, as long as you're online. Perfect for grinding against bots in Conquest Co-op.

</details>

<details id="openspy-alternatives">

<summary>Are there alternatives to OpenSpy?</summary>

A few, like [NovGames](https://novgames.ru/) and [PlayBF2142](http://play2142.ru/). But OpenSpy, with the Reclamation community, is by far the most active and reliable. If you want to try NovGames, BF2142 Hub can switch between the two.

</details>

## Steps

<Aside type="caution">

BF2142 Hub won't run on 32-bit Windows XP. On XP, follow [these steps](/getting-started/before-proceeding/#im-using-windows-xp-32-bit) instead.

</Aside>

<Aside type="note">

You may see an `mdIBF.DNS` error while applying the patches. If all four ticks turn <mark class="ui ui-green">green</mark>, you can ignore it.

</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Right-click the <mark class="ui">BF2142 Hub</mark> shortcut on your desktop → <mark class="ui">Properties</mark>.

1. On the <mark class="ui">Compatibility</mark> tab, tick <mark class="ui">Run this program as an administrator</mark>, then click <mark class="ui">OK</mark>. This avoids permission problems while patching.

1. Launch BF2142 Hub. If <mark class="ui">User Account Control</mark> asks, click <mark class="ui">Yes</mark>.

1. Open the <mark class="ui">Help</mark> tab (the question-mark icon) and check that <mark class="ui">GamePath</mark> points to your game folder. If not, click the gear icon to fix it.

1. In the <mark class="ui">Redirects\*</mark> drop-down, select <mark class="ui">OpenSpy</mark> and click <mark class="ui">Install</mark>. If an `mdIBF.DNS` error pops up, you can ignore it.

1. Confirm with <mark class="ui">Yes</mark>, then click <mark class="ui">Confirm</mark> when you see <mark class="ui">Patch completed, Enjoy!</mark>.

1. Check that `Patch 1.51`, `BF2142.exe`, `RendDX9.d` and `RendDX9ori.dll` all have <mark class="ui ui-green">green</mark> ticks. If not, repeat from step 5, or [install the patches manually](/help-centre/troubleshoot/#a05-install-openspy-patches-manually).

1. Next up: <GuideLink href="/getting-started/install-map-pack/" label="Install the map pack" />

   Only needed for the Reclamation servers. Otherwise, go straight to <GuideLink href="/getting-started/create-account/" label="Create an account" />

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">

The Hub's <mark class="ui">Help</mark> tab has troubleshooting and diagnostic tools for crashes, startup problems and graphics glitches. If the game looks off in windowed mode with Windows display scaling, [enable High DPI Aware](/help-centre/troubleshoot/#a01-enable-high-dpi-aware).

</Aside>

[^1]: Reclamation's servers adjust maps and modes to the number of players online. Titan matches start once at least 20 players are on the server.
