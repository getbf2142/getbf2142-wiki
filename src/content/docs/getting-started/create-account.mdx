The last step! Create your account and soldier, set up your video and audio, and you're ready to play.

<GuideSummary step={6} time="5 minutes">

Launch the game from the Hub, create an account and a soldier, then set your resolution and turn anti-aliasing off.

</GuideSummary>

## Steps

<Aside type="caution">

Whenever Windows Firewall asks, allow the game on both private and public networks, otherwise you may run into connection problems.

</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Launch the game through <mark class="ui">BF2142 Hub</mark>. You can tweak the video and game settings in the Hub before hitting <mark class="ui">Play</mark>.

1. Expect a short black screen before the intro, that's normal.[^1]

1. On the login screen, click <mark class="ui">CREATE NEW ACCOUNT</mark>, then <mark class="ui">ACCEPT TERMS</mark>. Each screen can take a few seconds to appear.[^2]

1. Fill in the registration form and click <mark class="ui">CREATE</mark>.

   * Use a real email address if you can, as you'll need it to [reset your password](/help-centre/faq/#how-to-reset-my-password).
   * Remember your <mark class="ui">ACCOUNT NAME</mark> and <mark class="ui">PASSWORD</mark>.
   * Your password is stored in plain text on the server, so use one you don't use anywhere else.
   * The other details don't really matter.

   Got an error? See [S01 in Troubleshoot](/help-centre/troubleshoot/#s01-account-creation-error-when-creating-an-account).

1. Click <mark class="ui">CREATE NEW SOLDIER</mark>, enter the name you want to use in-game, and confirm.

1. You'll probably notice the resolution has changed, since your new profile starts with default settings.[^3] Go to <mark class="ui">OPTIONS</mark> → <mark class="ui">VIDEO</mark> and set the resolution and refresh rate. _Only pick values your monitor supports_, as the wrong ones are the most common cause of crashes.

1. Tune the rest of your video settings. We recommend maxing everything out except <mark class="ui">ANTI-ALIASING</mark>, which is safest <mark class="ui">OFF</mark>.[^4] The [Tweak Guide](https://tweakguides.dmegaming.com/BF2142_6.html) explains every option.

1. Don't forget your <mark class="ui">AUDIO</mark> settings. The [Tweak Guide](https://tweakguides.dmegaming.com/BF2142_6.html) has great tips there too.

1. _**That's it, Battlefield 2142 is up and running. See you on the battlefield!**_

</Steps>

[^1]: In fullscreen, the game may flicker or resize a few times while starting. That's normal too.

[^2]: The master servers are in Europe and the US, so a response can take up to about 10 seconds.

[^3]: BF2142 keeps separate settings before and after you log in, and your new profile only has the defaults.

[^4]: On modern graphics cards, the old DirectX 9 anti-aliasing can cause glitches like seeing through buildings. If you want smoothing, turn it off in-game and force it in your NVIDIA or AMD control panel instead. No issues? Feel free to use <mark class="ui">4x</mark>.
