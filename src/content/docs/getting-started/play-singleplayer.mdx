There are two ways to play against bots: a quick game in <mark class="ui">SINGLEPLAY</mark>, or your own LAN server in <mark class="ui">MULTIPLAY</mark> for more control. Either way, once your game is [OpenSpy-ready](/getting-started/apply-openspy-patches/), the customization screen works and every unlock is available.

## Quick game in SINGLEPLAY

The fastest way in, though you can't change settings like tickets, team ratio, round time, friendly fire or respawn time.

<Steps>

1. Select <mark class="ui">SINGLEPLAY</mark>.

1. Pick your settings and click <mark class="ui">START PLAYLIST</mark>.

</Steps>

## Custom game in MULTIPLAY

Takes a few more clicks, but lets you tune tickets, team ratio, round time, friendly fire, respawn time and more.

<Steps>

1. Select <mark class="ui">MULTIPLAY</mark> → <mark class="ui">LOCAL</mark> → <mark class="ui">CREATE</mark>.

1. Pick your settings and click <mark class="ui">START SERVER</mark>.

</Steps>

<details id="the-game-will-start-when-1-more-players-join">

<summary>Stuck on "The game will start when 1 more player(s) join"?</summary>

Add this server setting. The [Server Settings](/advanced/addons-tweaks/server-settings/) guide shows you how:

```batch
sv.numPlayersNeededToStart 1
```

</details>

Want even more control, like tickets or respawn time in SINGLEPLAY? See [Server Settings](/advanced/addons-tweaks/server-settings/).
