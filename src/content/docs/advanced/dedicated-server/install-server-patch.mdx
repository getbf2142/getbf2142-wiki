Just like the game, your server needs the OpenSpy patch to go online. ([Why?](/getting-started/apply-openspy-patches/))

<GuideSummary step={2} total={3} time="5 minutes">

Move `BF2142_w32ded.exe` next to the patcher, run it as administrator, click <mark class="ui">Open & Verify</mark> then <mark class="ui">Patch!</mark> until both ticks are green, and move the file back.

</GuideSummary>

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Download the server patch from [Downloads](#downloads) and unzip it to your desktop.

1. Move `BF2142_w32ded.exe` from `C:\Program Files (x86)\Electronic Arts\Battlefield 2142 Server` into the unzipped folder.

1. In that folder, right-click `BF2142_Server_Patch.exe` → <mark class="ui">Run as administrator</mark>. If <mark class="ui">User Account Control</mark> asks, click <mark class="ui">Yes</mark>.

1. Click <mark class="ui">Open & Verify</mark>, select `BF2142_w32ded.exe`, and wait for the step 1 tick to turn green.

1. Click <mark class="ui">Patch!</mark> and wait for the step 2 tick to turn green.

1. Move `BF2142_w32ded.exe` back to `C:\Program Files (x86)\Electronic Arts\Battlefield 2142 Server`.

1. Next up: <GuideLink href="/advanced/dedicated-server/host-dedicated-server/" label="Host a dedicated server" />

</Steps>
