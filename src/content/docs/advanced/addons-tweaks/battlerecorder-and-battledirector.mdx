Relive your best BF2142 moments from a whole new angle! The game's built-in _BattleRecorder_ records your matches, and _BattleDirector_ lets you replay them with a free camera, perfect for highlights and cinematic shots.

## Setting up

<Steps>

1. Create a `Demos` folder in `Battlefield 2142\mods\<MOD>`, where `<MOD>` is `bf2142` for vanilla or your mod's folder.

1. Download and install BattleDirector from [Downloads](#downloads).

1. Open BattleDirector and give its <mark class="ui">Quick Help</mark> a read.

1. In <mark class="ui">Settings</mark>, set <mark class="ui">Game</mark> to <mark class="ui">Battlefield 2142</mark> and <mark class="ui">Game EXE Location</mark> to your `BF2142.exe`.

1. Your recordings appear in the <mark class="ui">Launcher</mark> tab once you select the right <mark class="ui">Mod</mark>.

</Steps>

## Recording a match

### In singleplayer or LAN

<Steps>

1. In-game, press `~` to open the console.

1. Type `demo.recordDemo NAME` and press `Enter`. You'll see `recordingDemo` if it worked.

1. To stop, type `demo.stopRecording` and press `Enter`, or just let the match end.

1. Your demo is saved in `Battlefield 2142\mods\<MOD>\Demos`.

</Steps>

### In multiplayer

<Aside type="note">

Reclamation has turned off in-game demo downloads. Ask an admin on their [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/MEwBW9U) for the file, with the time of your match.

</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Join a server with BattleRecorder enabled.

1. After the round, go to <mark class="ui">Community</mark> → <mark class="ui">Battle Recorder</mark> and download the demo under <mark class="ui">Bookmarks</mark>. It's saved to `Documents\Battlefield 2142\Profiles\Default\demos`.

1. Copy it to `Battlefield 2142\mods\<MOD>\Demos` to watch it in BattleDirector.

</Steps>

With the OpenSpy patches installed, demos don't show up in the game's own Battle Recorder library, so use BattleDirector to watch them.

## Watching a match

<Steps>

1. Start BattleDirector.

1. In <mark class="ui">Settings</mark>, set <mark class="ui">FPS</mark>, <mark class="ui">FOV</mark>, <mark class="ui">Hide Nametags</mark> and <mark class="ui">Hide HUD</mark> to your liking, and choose your <mark class="ui">Resolution</mark> (tick <mark class="ui">Windowed</mark> if needed).

   For true widescreen, add `+widescreen 1` to the second input box, along with any [other launch options](/advanced/addons-tweaks/custom-shortcuts/) you like.

1. In the <mark class="ui">Launcher</mark> tab, select the <mark class="ui">Mod</mark> the demo was recorded with, pick the demo and click <mark class="ui">Record New Track</mark>.

1. When the map has loaded, click <mark class="ui">JOIN GAME</mark>.

</Steps>

## Controls

* `Q` opens the Play Rose for start, pause, speed and rewind. _Don't_ press Restart, as it breaks the recording.
* `T` opens the Camera Rose to switch players, or toggle between free and player camera.

**Playback**

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `1` | Pause |
| `2` / `6` | Normal speed |
| `3` | 5% speed |
| `4` | 25% speed |
| `5` | 50% speed |
| `7` | 150% speed |
| `8` | 300% speed |

**Camera**

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Space` / `Shift + Space` | Next / previous player |
| Right mouse button | Toggle free camera and player camera |
| Mouse wheel | Zoom while following a player |
| `W` `A` `S` `D` | Move the free camera |
| `Ctrl` / `Shift` | Move the free camera down / up |

**Console commands**

| Command | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `renderer.drawHud 0` / `1` | Hide / show the HUD |
| `demo.adjustDemoFov 90` | Change the FOV, try 40 - 50 for cinematic shots |
| `demo.ShutdownDemo` | Exit the demo |

## Known issues

* BattleDirector won't start and says "See the logfile ... for details"? Install the [Visual C++ 2003 runtime](/help-centre/troubleshoot/#a06-install-microsoft-visual-c-2003-redistributable).
* Player gestures (like "Thank you" from the Q menu) don't play back.
* Recording can pause or crash when the host player dies.
* AVI exports usually only work in windowed mode, as fullscreen AVIs can be corrupt.
* AVI files over 4 GB break. Keep clips small, or record with OBS instead. For example, 1520×855 (3.6 GB) works, while 1744×981 (4.1 GB) doesn't.
