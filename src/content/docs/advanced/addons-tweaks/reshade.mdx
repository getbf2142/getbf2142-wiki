ReShade adds a layer of post-processing shaders on top of the game, giving the visuals a big boost. It first became popular in Project Reality for BF2, and the Project Remaster team adapted it for BF2142.

<div class="figure-row"><figure><img src="/assets/reshade_remagen.webp" alt="ReShade on Breakthrough at Remagen" loading="lazy" /><figcaption><p>Breakthrough at Remagen</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="/assets/reshade_street.webp" alt="ReShade on Street" loading="lazy" /><figcaption><p>Street</p></figcaption></figure></div>

The latest ReShade setup comes built into [Project Remaster](/advanced/project-remaster/install-project-remaster/). Don't use the mod? This guide shows you how to add it to vanilla BF2142 too. (The [original ModDB release](https://www.moddb.com/downloads/bf2142-reshade) is outdated.)

## Steps

<Aside type="tip">

**Using Project Remaster?** Just tick <mark class="ui">Reshade</mark> in the Remaster Launcher's <mark class="ui">Settings</mark> tab and jump to [Using ReShade](#using-reshade). Untick it to turn it off again.

</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Download the patch from [Downloads](#downloads).

1. Extract everything into your game folder, usually `C:\Program Files (x86)\Electronic Arts\Battlefield 2142`, and overwrite any files.

1. Installed the game somewhere else? Open `d3d9.ini` with a text editor and update the paths to match.

</Steps>

## Using ReShade

<Steps>

1. Launch the game. You'll see the effect as soon as the intro or login screen appears.

1. Press <mark class="ui">Shift + F2</mark> to open or close the ReShade overlay.

1. On the <mark class="ui">Home</mark> tab, check that the preset has loaded. You should see shaders like `Levels.fx` and `Vignette.fx`.

   Empty list? The shaders haven't loaded yet:

   1. On the <mark class="ui">Settings</mark> tab, set the paths (adjust if your game is elsewhere):
      * Effect search path: `C:\Program Files (x86)\Electronic Arts\Battlefield 2142\reshade-shaders\Shaders`
      * Texture search path: `C:\Program Files (x86)\Electronic Arts\Battlefield 2142\reshade-shaders\Textures`
   2. On the <mark class="ui">Home</mark> tab, click <mark class="ui">Reload</mark>.
   3. Check that the preset drop-down points to `...\Battlefield 2142\DefaultPreset.ini`.

1. Toggle the effect on and off with <mark class="ui">Scroll Lock</mark>. We like rebinding it to <mark class="ui">Shift + F1</mark> in the overlay.

1. To fine-tune the shaders, enable <mark class="ui">Configuration Mode</mark> on the <mark class="ui">Settings</mark> tab, then adjust them on the <mark class="ui">Home</mark> tab.

1. Want your own look? Click <mark class="ui">+</mark> next to the preset drop-down to create a preset. The download also includes a second one, `DefaultPreset2`, to try.

1. Seeing odd colours on some maps? Try turning off the LUT shader.

</Steps>

## Good to know

* Needs Windows 7 or newer.
* Works with every mod, including vanilla.
* Safe for multiplayer, you won't get kicked for using it.
* To uninstall, delete the files you added.
