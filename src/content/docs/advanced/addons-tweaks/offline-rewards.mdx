Earn pins, ribbons, badges and medals against bots! This patch brings the ranked-server rewards to your singleplayer and LAN co-op games, and there's nothing quite like racking up a pile of medals in one round.

In vanilla, many awards need things like 150 hours played or 300 EU wins, which you'll never reach in a single round. So we reworked them for quick co-op games:

* Titan mode rewards are removed.
* Impossible requirements are gone.
* Most rewards can be earned in about 15 minutes.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Download the patch from [Downloads](#downloads).

1. Open your game folder, where `BF2142.exe` lives, usually `C:\Program Files (x86)\Electronic Arts\Battlefield 2142`.

1. Rename the existing `python` folder to `python_o` as a backup.

1. Copy the `python` folder from the `.zip` into your game folder.

</Steps>

## Good to know

* Works with every mod, including vanilla.
* Only affects singleplayer and co-op. Multiplayer is untouched, and you won't get kicked for having it installed.
* Rewards only last for the current round and reset when it ends.
* Want logs? Edit `python\bf2\__init__.py` and change `g_debug`, `g_debug_log` and `g_falog`.
