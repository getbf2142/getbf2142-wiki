Love the chat from BF3 and BF4? This addon brings that clean, modern look to BF2142, for soldiers and commanders alike.

## Before you start

* Install the [Widescreen HUD fix](/advanced/addons-tweaks/hudfix/) first. Using Project Remaster? Just enable <mark class="ui">HUD-fix</mark> in the Remaster Launcher.
* Your game folder is where `BF2142.exe` lives, usually `C:\Program Files (x86)\Electronic Arts\Battlefield 2142`.
* The addon goes into `mods\bf2142` for vanilla, or into your mod's folder.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Download the addon from [Downloads](#downloads), with or without the chat background.

1. Follow the steps in the included `README.txt`.

## Good to know

* Undo the changes before joining Reclamation or other online servers, unless the server uses this addon too.
* Hosting with this addon? Players who join need it too.
* To uninstall, undo the steps from the README.
