## At a glance

| Source | What you get | Playable on Reclamation servers? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Vanilla BF2142](#vanilla-bf2142) | 5 maps, 16 players | Yes |
| [Project Remaster](#project-remaster) | 20+ maps in many sizes, with smarter bots | No, Remaster only |
| [Reclamation map pack](#reclamation-map-pack) | A handful of maps with bot support | Yes |
| [ModDB](#moddb) | 2 extra community maps | No |
| [GetBF2142](#getbf2142) | 6 fixed-up custom maps | No |

The number after each map is the player size of its bot-supported layouts.

## Vanilla BF2142

Five maps support bots out of the box, all at 16 players:

* Belgrade
* Cerbere Landing
* Fall of Berlin
* Suez Canal
* Verdun

## Project Remaster

The best option by far: bot support on many more maps and layouts, smarter AI, better textures and balance, and even partial Titan support in the 48-player layout.

## GetBF2142

We've fixed and tweaked a few maps from the Reclamation map pack so they play properly with bots. Most are custom maps and classics from other Battlefield games.

<Aside type="note">

To install a map, extract it into `mods\<MOD>\Levels` in your game folder.[^6] These versions aren't compatible with the Reclamation servers.

</Aside>

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Street">

A BF2 community map by spfreak, set in a Middle Eastern town: a linear string of control points with plenty of urban cover for intense street fights. Solid bot support makes it a blast.

<Embed url="https://www.mediafire.com/file/3433du00q3m66aj/Street.zip/file" title="Street">

Source: [GetBF2142](/)

</Embed>

<Embed url="https://drive.google.com/file/d/13KGr74VHJZ0cEUhrWTj0lILPQYoCEedl" title="Street">

Source: [GetBF2142](/)

</Embed>

<details>

<summary>What's changed</summary>

* Removed the "ghost car", a broken object that could get players stuck.
* Renamed control points and spawn points for clarity.

</details>

**Credits:** spfreak and TGW for porting it from BF2. The original is in the Reclamation map pack.

</TabItem>

<TabItem label="Desert Storm">

A wide-open desert with strong BF1 Sinai Desert vibes. Every control point is packed with vehicles, perfect for big tank and walker battles.

<Embed url="https://www.mediafire.com/file/3s8h1tlw0qkj3vq/Desert_Storm.zip/file" title="Desert Storm">

Source: [GetBF2142](/)

</Embed>

<Embed url="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1R4RM4BgTphDS1MHi7UOybneauD9Fd_IN" title="Desert Storm">

Source: [GetBF2142](/)

</Embed>

<details>

<summary>What's changed</summary>

* Fixed the sky brightness and colour saturation for a Belgrade-like look that's easier on the eyes.
* More walkers, tanks and AGs, so most bots can jump into a vehicle.

</details>

**Credits:** Jeff & Robin, the map's creators (as far as we know). The original is in the Reclamation map pack.

</TabItem>

<TabItem label="Victory Village">

The well-known BF Heroes map, now in vanilla BF2142 with decent bot support. Playable, with a few quirks.

[yone's Victory Village 2142](https://www.moddb.com/games/battlefield-2142/addons/na16686) had bot support but often crashed on newer game versions, while [matthysjordaan's Victory Village (Fixed)](https://www.moddb.com/addons/victory-village-fixed) loaded fine but lost the bots. We combined the two and added our own tweaks.

<Embed url="https://www.mediafire.com/file/1izdiine7zys8gg/Victory_Village.zip/file" title="Victory Village">

Source: [GetBF2142](/)

</Embed>

<Embed url="https://drive.google.com/file/d/16tQTpHsVWRzDdANgcCxDBuu5PWxNt3Qi" title="Victory Village">

Source: [GetBF2142](/)

</Embed>

<details>

<summary>What's changed</summary>

* Restored bot support.
* Removed buggy spawn points where bots died instantly.
* Cleaned up unnecessary files and dependencies.
* Both team bases can't be captured, and the three central points start neutral.

</details>

**Credits:** [yone](https://www.moddb.com/members/yone) and [matthysjordaan](https://www.moddb.com/members/matthysjordaan) for porting it from Battlefield Heroes. The originals are on ModDB and in the Reclamation map pack.

</TabItem>

<TabItem label="Sharqi Peninsula">

The classic BF2 map, famous for its construction-site control point, fixed up for bots.

<Embed url="https://www.mediafire.com/file/67r7vcjkkilg1rw/tgw_sharqi_peninsula.zip/file" title="Sharqi Peninsula">

Source: [GetBF2142](/)

</Embed>

<Embed url="https://drive.google.com/file/d/11TrHvUL2v9573yHqbN0KtgP07KTkg-5_" title="Sharqi Peninsula">

Source: [GetBF2142](/)

</Embed>

<details>

<summary>What's changed</summary>

* No more crash in singleplayer or co-op, as BF2 vehicles are swapped for BF2142 ones.
* Adjusted the lighting so the map isn't overly dark.

</details>

Bots still struggle with the stairs at the last control point, but it's very playable.

**Credits:** TGW for porting it from BF2. The original is in the Reclamation map pack.

</TabItem>

<TabItem label="Operation Amos">

The popular BF2 map, fixed up for bots.

<Embed url="https://www.mediafire.com/file/8wecb31wlmggf8a/tgw_operation_amos.zip/file" title="Operation Amos">

Source: [GetBF2142](/)

</Embed>

<Embed url="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Hn28cacrI1D5QX2d6ACmf1PpD1yp70Ue" title="Operation Amos">

Source: [GetBF2142](/)

<details>

<summary>What's changed</summary>

* No more crash in singleplayer or co-op, as BF2 vehicles are swapped for BF2142 ones.

</details>

Bots still struggle with vehicles at the last control point, but it's very playable.

**Credits:** BF:A Ha-Knomboe Boy and TGW for porting it from BF2. The original is in the Reclamation map pack.

<TabItem label="Wall of Jericho">

Known as Great Wall in BF2, a classic map set around a walled city in China.

<Embed url="https://www.mediafire.com/file/oisy546dfqfry72/Wall_of_Jericho.zip/file" title="Wall of Jericho">

Source: [GetBF2142](/)

</Embed>

<Embed url="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1WvAlmqDOlT1_tBm_sbnMqbJsC7hxF6VK" title="Wall of Jericho">

Source: [GetBF2142](/)

</Embed>

<details>

<summary>What's changed</summary>

* No more crash in singleplayer or co-op, as BF2 vehicles are swapped for BF2142 ones.
* Added bot support using the AI and pathfinding files from the original BF2 map.

</details>

Bots sometimes get stuck inside the walled city, so bot play is just okay. It's still fun though, and a great spot for screenshots.

**Credits:** TGW for porting it from BF2. The original is in the Reclamation map pack.
