For many players, Project Remaster is now the only way to play BF2142. It gives this 2006 classic a modern makeover with sharper visuals, new weapons and much smarter bots, and it's easy to install.

<GuideSummary step={1} total={2} time="10 minutes">

Needs the v1.51 patch. Download the mod, extract it into your game's `mods` folder and put a Remaster Launcher shortcut on your desktop. Playing on OpenSpy? Create a new soldier just for Remaster.

</GuideSummary>

<Embed url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WnaceSKVyH4" />

<details id="overview">

<summary>What is Project Remaster?</summary>

A huge community mod that overhauls BF2142's visuals, weapons and AI while keeping the game you know. It's made by the [Project Remaster team](https://prmp.boards.net/). Join their [Discord](https://discord.gg/nVdDkgA) for news and support.

</details>

<details id="features">

<summary>What's in it?</summary>

* Overhauled textures for soldiers, weapons, vehicles, terrain and maps
* A sharper font, HUD, crosshairs, minimap and menus for modern widescreen displays
* New weapons, including sniper rifles, launchers, machine guns and pistols
* Up to 5 primary weapons per class, new sidearms and a Squad Leader gadget slot
* New and updated animations, and realistic explosion effects
* Underwater swimming, hold `Z` to dive
* Smarter bots that fight over longer distances, capture objectives and use vehicles
* Bot support on many more maps and layouts ([see the list](/advanced/addons-tweaks/maps-with-bots/#project-remaster)), plus limited Titan mode for 48-player singleplayer and co-op
* New maps, including Backstab, Operation Imra, Battle for Frankfurt and Tunis Harbour Assault
* Built-in [ReShade](/advanced/addons-tweaks/reshade/), [widescreen HUD fix](/advanced/addons-tweaks/hudfix/) and blood patch
* A launcher with a manual and troubleshooting tools

The full list is in the [v17 release notes](https://www.moddb.com/mods/bf2142-project-remaster/news/project-remaster-update-v17).

</details>

<details id="official-server">

<summary>Can I play it online?</summary>

Yes, the mod fully supports multiplayer over LAN, VLAN or the internet. Everyone needs the same version of the mod, and joining a vanilla server with it loaded gets you kicked.

There are no public v17 servers yet, but that's perfect for hosting your own [LAN party](/getting-started/host-server/). Keep an eye on the [Remaster Discord](https://discord.gg/nVdDkgA) for official servers and events.

</details>

## Before you start

* Your game must be patched to [v1.51](/getting-started/download-and-install-v1_51-patch/).
* Playing on OpenSpy through BF2142 Hub? **Create a new soldier just for Remaster.** Using your vanilla soldier can break the mod's expanded kit customization and crash the game when you spawn.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Download the mod from [Downloads](#downloads).

1. Extract it into your game's `mods` folder, usually\
   `C:\Program Files (x86)\Electronic Arts\Battlefield 2142\mods`

   Updating from an older version? Delete the old mod's folder first.

1. Open `mods\Project_Remaster_v17\Tools\Launcher`, right-click `Remaster_Launcher.exe` and choose <mark class="ui">Send to</mark> → <mark class="ui">Desktop (create shortcut)</mark>.

1. Next up: <GuideLink href="/advanced/project-remaster/configure-remaster-launcher/" label="Launch Project Remaster" />

</Steps>
