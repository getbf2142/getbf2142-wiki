## Why we created GetBF2142

Since GameSpy shut down in 2014, getting into Battlefield 2142, online or solo, has been a real puzzle. GetBF2142 exists to fix that.

We're a fan-run project: no ads, no revenue, just people who love this game and want it to be easy for everyone to play again.

## How we support the community

The community needs players, so we focus on getting you into the game with as little friction as possible, through clear guides and reliable downloads.

We're independent and not tied to any other community project, so if something ever goes wrong on our side, it doesn't affect anyone else.

## What you can expect

* **Clear, detailed guides,** step by step, with the "why" explained when it matters.
* **Safe downloads,** tested and mirrored, so you're not hunting through dead links.
* **Extra help where you need it,** with tips, fixes and notes right where they're useful.
* **Kept up to date,** as we review the guides regularly as tools and mods change.

## Keeping BF2142 alive, responsibly

We don't support piracy and have great respect for EA and DICE. The game has been abandoned with no official way to play it today, so we simply help fans keep it alive.
